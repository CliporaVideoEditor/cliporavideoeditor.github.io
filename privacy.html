Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. Here's how Clipora handles information when you use the service.

Last updated: August 28, 2026
Information How We Use It Storage Third Parties Deletion
01

Introduction

Welcome to Clipora. Clipora is a video editing project designed to provide creators with a simple and accessible way to create and edit videos.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use Clipora and how that information may be handled.

02

Information We May Collect

Depending on the features available in Clipora, we may collect information that you provide when creating or using an account.

  • Account information such as a username and email address.
  • Information needed to authenticate your account.
  • Preferences and settings associated with your Clipora account.
  • Information you voluntarily provide when contacting Clipora.
03

How We Use Information

Information collected by Clipora may be used to provide, maintain, and improve the service.

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Provide Clipora's editing features.
  • Save user preferences.
  • Protect the service from abuse.
  • Diagnose technical problems.
  • Improve Clipora's features and user experience.
04

Your Projects & Media

Clipora may allow you to work with videos, images, audio, and other media.

How your media is stored depends on which Clipora features are available and whether a project is stored locally on your device or through Clipora's online services.

Important We will clearly explain online storage features as they are introduced.
05

Local Storage & Cookies

Clipora may use browser technologies such as local storage to remember settings, preferences, or temporary application data.

These technologies may allow the website to remember things such as interface preferences or information needed for the application to function.

06

Third-Party Services

Clipora may use third-party services to provide certain functionality, such as authentication, hosting, analytics, or infrastructure.

When third-party services are used, their own privacy policies may also apply to information they process.

We will update this policy when significant third-party services are added to Clipora.

07

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by Clipora. However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

If we become aware of a significant security issue affecting users, we will take appropriate steps to address it.

08

Account & Data Deletion

Clipora is intended to provide users with control over their accounts and information.

Account deletion and data-management options will be provided as the corresponding account systems are implemented.

09

Children's Privacy

Clipora is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

If you believe information has been collected improperly, please contact the Clipora team so the situation can be reviewed.

10

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Clipora develops new features or changes how information is handled.

When changes are made, the updated date at the top of this page will be changed accordingly.

11

Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact the Clipora team through the official Clipora project channels.
